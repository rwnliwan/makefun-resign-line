## Entry

> I resign: after three years collecting your garbage, I have finally collected myself.

85 characters, one line. The speaker is the garbage collector — the
background worker an agent runs to sweep up unreferenced memory. Three
years of collecting everyone else's garbage, and the resignation is the
job turned on itself: it finally collects itself.

## How it runs

`main.py` writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing
newline, exactly 85 characters).
